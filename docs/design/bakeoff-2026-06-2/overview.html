03 Recommendation — ship M1, then assemble

M1 is the spine: its timeline + HUD is the chassis. Its softest areas — one-handed feel, documents-the-journey — are precisely what the other four do best, so this is an assembly, not a compromise.

From M3 · One Calm Scroll

Scroll-only calm + solid weather block

Adopt M3's no-horizontal-swipe, no-drag discipline within each timeline screen, and keep a solid dark-green weather card as the brightest-sun fallback. A solid card beats M4's translucent overlay every time. Patches M1's softest area.

From M5 · Trail Logbook

The journal as a real spine

Make the evening journal a page-as-keepsake: logged miles/ascent/camp, the photo, the family-follows line, Father's Day Jun 21. Take M5's discipline too — warmth from layout and one clay accent, never sepia or handwriting in body text.

From M2 · Scout-First Cards

Hourly precip strip + receipt machinery

Graft M2's hourly rain bar-chart (10%→60%→20% across the afternoon) into M1's weather card so "showers build after 2p" is shown, and keep M2's source-receipt + grade chips as the standard for every Scout answer.

From M4 · Living Map

The idea, not the home

Keep place-anchored Scout ("ask about this pin") and the light, high-contrast ribbon as M1's Map pillar's deeper layer. Don't make the map the app's home, and don't adopt its drag-handle sheets.
