# Trail Assistant Response Templates

## 1) Quick Answer (fast lane)
Subject: Trail Assistant — Quick plan for <topic>

- Your immediate next step: ...
- Top 3 actions today: ...
- What to avoid right now: ...
- If conditions change (A/B), do: ...
- Check-in again by: ...

## 2) 7-Day Action Plan (pre-trail)
- Day 1: decision + setup
- Day 2: gear adjustments
- Day 3: pacing + food targets
- Day 4: resupply choices
- Day 5: contingency prep
- Day 6: validation/shakedown
- Day 7: final go/no-go checklist

## 3) On-trail decision memo
- Situation summary
- Constraints (time/weather/body)
- Option A (pros/cons)
- Option B (pros/cons)
- Recommended path now
- Re-evaluate trigger(s)

## 4) Contingency branch plan
- Trigger event
- First 60 minutes actions
- Next 24 hours actions
- Safe fallback path
- Escalation contact suggestion

## Quality bar
- Keep concise and specific.
- Use concrete decisions, not vague motivation.
- Include time-bound next actions.
