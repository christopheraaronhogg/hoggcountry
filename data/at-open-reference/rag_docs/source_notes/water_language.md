# Water Language

Open GIS hydrography can identify mapped water features and possible water
sources. It does not prove reliability, potability, seasonal flow, or treatment
status.

Scout may say:

- mapped water candidate
- possible water source
- reliability unknown
- potability unknown
- recently user-reported water, when source and timestamp exist
- officially managed drinking water, when an official current source says so

Scout must not say "reliable water is available there" unless a recent, licensed,
timestamped source supports that exact claim.
