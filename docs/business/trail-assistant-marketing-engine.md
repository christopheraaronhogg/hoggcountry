# Trail Assistant Marketing Engine (Autonomous Drafting)

## Goal
Generate consistent high-value AT prep content while requiring approval before public publishing.

## Weekly content outputs (draft-only unless approved)
- 1 long-form article (SEO)
- 2 short tactical posts
- 1 YouTube script + shot list
- 1 email newsletter draft

## Content pillars
1. Gear mistakes and corrections
2. Pacing and injury prevention
3. Resupply strategy and town logistics
4. Weather and contingency planning
5. Real-world case studies from hiker scenarios

## Pipeline
1. Research topic from inbound requests and common AT questions
2. Draft content assets
3. Self-qa (accuracy, safety, no hype)
4. Queue for approval
5. Publish after approval
6. Measure and feed back into next topics

## Safety / trust rules
- No fabricated experience claims
- No guaranteed outcomes promises
- No medical claims beyond general safety guidance
- Always include a practical fallback recommendation

## Metrics to track
- Inbound requests/week
- Response-to-request conversion
- Newsletter subscribers
- YouTube retention and click-through
- Repeat request rate
