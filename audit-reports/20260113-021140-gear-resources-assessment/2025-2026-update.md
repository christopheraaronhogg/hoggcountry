## Executive Summary: What Changed

The 2025 AT thru-hiker survey reveals **significant market shifts** that require updating your BudgetGearBuilder recommendations:

### Major Market Shifts

1. **Durston Gear dethroned Zpacks** as most popular shelter brand
2. **Durston X-Mid 1 became #1 tent model** (Gen 3 redesign in 2025)
3. **Hammock usage doubled** year-over-year (to 9%)
4. **Altra regained #1 footwear spot** from Topo Athletic
5. **Budget tier floor raised** from $383 to $400 (inflation)
6. **Mid-range centered on Durston** (X-Mid + Kakwa combo)

## Updated Budget Tiers (2025/2026)

### Budget Tier: $400-700 (was $383-600)

**Inflation adjustment:** Entry point rose from $383 to $400-600 range

**Key products:**
- 3F UL Lanshan 1 Pro: Still budget king ($120, 26oz)
- Hammock Gear Econ Burrow: Now $160 (was $150)
- Nemo Switchback CCF: $50 (up from ~$45)
- Nitecore NU25: $40 (up from $35)

**Complete build:** ~$605 for 9.5 lb base weight

---

### Mid-Range Tier: $1,000-$1,800 (No change)

**MAJOR SHIFT:** Durston products now dominate recommendations

**2024 recommendations:**
- Shelter: Tarptent Double Rainbow, various
- Pack: Granite Gear Crown3, ULA Circuit, Gossamer Mariposa
- Quilt: UGQ Bandit, EE Revelation

**2025 recommendations:**
- Shelter: **Durston X-Mid 1 Gen 3** ($250, 25oz) — #1 AT tent
- Pack: **Durston Kakwa 55** ($280, 31oz) — best value-to-performance
- Quilt: UGQ Bandit or EE Revelation (no change)

**Complete build:** ~$1,325 for 10 lb base weight

**Why Durston dominates:**
- X-Mid 1 Gen 3 completely redesigned for 2025 (lighter, more spacious)
- Became most popular shelter brand per Trek survey
- Kakwa 55 offers best value-to-performance for ultralight pack

---

### Premium Tier: $2,500-$3,500+ (No change)

**No major shifts** — Zpacks, HMG, EE still lead premium tier

**Notable:**
- Durston X-Mid Pro 1 DCF ($499, 13.5oz) — top-rated premium shelter
- Zpacks still dominant in DCF market (Duplex, Arc Haul)
- EE Enigma 950fp remains premium quilt standard
